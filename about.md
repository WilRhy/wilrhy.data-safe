# Data Safe (WORK IN PROGRESS)
Data Safe is a mod that saves your data to your GD/Boomlings account and locally into the mod's folder. This mod is still currently in development and a work in progress.

## Config Menu
Just click the gear at the bottom left corner to pull up the config menu and turn off notifications, test notifications and syncing, import and export files, and allow constant saving to your GD account.

# How does it work?
It works just by working. It gives you a notification giving a countdown when it saves. This can be disabled in config menu. The mod GDrive Backup is required if you want to save data to your Google Drive instead of saving locally onto your computer into your files.

# What happens if I exit the game while its saving?
It will restrain you from fully closing the game to finish saving. Then, it will allow you to exit the game. If you shut down your computer while its saving when Geometry Dash is closing, it may have bugs. I have not tested it myself.

# Compatibility
I have not checked if this works with Mac or Linux considering that I don't own a Mac nor have a computer with Linux installed.
